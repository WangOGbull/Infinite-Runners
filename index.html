HOW TO PLAY

Mobile: Move

Touch and drag anywhere to guide your dragon.

Mobile: Sprint

Tap the SPRINT button for a 5-second speed burst. Requires 20 ∞ food to charge.

Mobile: Attack

Tap the ATTACK button when charged to enable kill collisions. Requires 20 ∞ food to charge.

PC

Move = Mouse, Sprint = Space, Pause = ESC

Head vs Head

Bigger dragon bounces the smaller one away. To actually KILL on head-on-head, your ATTACK meter must be charged. No attack = no kill, just a bump.

Head vs Tail

Hit a bigger dragon's tail = you lose segments (tail cut). Hit a smaller dragon's tail with ATTACK active = massive damage. Equal size = bounce.

Head vs Body

Hitting a dragon's mid-body does nothing — you pass through. Only head and tail contacts matter.

Sprint

Eat 20 ∞ food to fully charge. Tap SPRINT for a 5-second speed burst. No auto-regen — you earn every charge by eating.

Attack

Eat 20 ∞ food to charge ATTACK. When active, head-on-head kills smaller dragons and tail hits deal 30% segment damage. Without it, collisions just bounce.

Growth Stages

Hatchling (5 segments) → Drake (10) → Wyrm (15) → Ancient (25) → Max (50). Eat ∞ food to grow bigger and stronger.

3 Lives

Each dragon has 3 lives. Die 3 times and you're eliminated from the round.

Small Dragon Protection

Dragons under 8 segments (Hatchling) are fragile — bigger dragons can kill them even without ATTACK charged. Grow fast to survive.
